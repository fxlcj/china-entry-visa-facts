# China Entry, Visa & Border Rules — verified facts, with sources and check dates

A reference for the entry rules foreign visitors most often get wrong: who can come in without a visa and for how long, what the 240-hour transit cover, how arrival cards and registration work, and what each visa type means.

Every figure below carries **the official Chinese source that states it** and **the date it was last checked**. If you copy a number anywhere, copy the check date with it — that is what makes the number verifiable at all.

- `facts.json` — the same 31 entries, machine-readable
- The human-readable version, with worked examples: <https://jiunotes.top/en/>
- How entries are kept current: see [Maintenance](#maintenance)

## Why this exists

English-language guides are usually written once, from memory, and then age silently. Figures get quoted from a rule that changed, or recycled from another country. The rules and prices are not secret — they are published in Chinese by the authorities or the sites themselves. The gap is that almost nobody ties the English sentence back to the source, or says when they last looked. This file exists to close that gap.

**Scope, and a note on honesty.** These are published rules and prices. They are not travel advice, they are not a booking service, and this is not an official publication. Where a figure could not be confirmed against an official or site-operated page, it is not listed here — a shorter list beats a guessed number.

- Live visa fees by country on a live rate card — those are set per nationality by reciprocity and change; each row here cites the page that states the figure.
- Country-by-country passport validity or photo-spec pages — different authority, different change cadence.
- Anything we could not verify. If no official Chinese page states it, it is not here.

## Maintenance

This dataset is not written once and abandoned:

- Every source URL is re-fetched on a monthly maintenance run. When a page's content changes, the entry is flagged and the claim/figure is updated against the new official text with a CHANGELOG line.
- Each entry carries a `recheck_by` date in `facts.json`. Entries approaching that date surface as a re-check task.
- If a figure changes, its `checked_on` date changes with it. A number is never quietly swapped while the old date stays in place.
